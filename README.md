# 🗺️ CS50.AI Study Roadmap Summary

## **Phase 1: The Foundations (Search & Logic)**
*Focus: How AI solves problems using rules and exploration.*
- **Lecture 0: Artificial Intelligence**
  - Concepts: What is AI? History, Turing Test, Intelligent Agents.
- **Lecture 1: Search**
  - **Key Algorithms**: DFS, BFS, Greedy Best-First, A* Search.
  - **Application**: Solving mazes, pathfinding, game states.
  - **Project 1**: Degrees of Separation (Six Degrees of Kevin Bacon) or Tic-Tac-Toe.
- **Lecture 2: Knowledge & Logic**
  - **Key Concepts**: Propositional Logic, First-Order Logic, Inference Rules (Modus Ponens), Model Checking.
  - **Application**: Minesweeper solver (logic-based).
  - **Project 2**: Minesweeper AI.

> ✅ **Status**: You likely have a solid grasp here. These are the "classical" AI building blocks.

---

## **Phase 2: The Core Engine (Machine Learning & Neural Networks)**
*Focus: How AI learns from data instead of following hard-coded rules.*
- **Lecture 3: Uncertainty & Probability**
  - **Key Concepts**: Bayes' Theorem, Markov Models, Hidden Markov Models (HMM).
  - **Application**: PageRank, Spam filtering, Speech recognition basics.
- **Lecture 4: Optimization**
  - **Key Concepts**: Local Search, Hill Climbing, Simulated Annealing, Linear Programming.
  - **Application**: Scheduling problems, maximizing efficiency.
- **Lecture 5: Neural Networks (⭐ Your Current Focus)**
  - **Key Concepts**: Perceptrons, Activation Functions (ReLU, Sigmoid), Loss Functions, **Backpropagation**, Gradient Descent.
  - **Architectures**:
    - **Multilayer Perceptrons (MLP)**: Basic deep learning.
    - **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)**: For images (Kernels, Pooling).
    - **Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)**: For sequences (Memory, LSTM).
  - **Project 3**: Traffic Sign Recognition (using CNNs) or Handwriting Recognition.

> ✅ **Status**: This is the most critical part for modern AI. Mastering **Backpropagation**, **CNNs**, and **RNNs** here gives you the power to build 90% of current AI applications.

---

## **Phase 3: Advanced Topics (Optional/Skip for Now)**
*Focus: Specialized domains requiring heavy theory.*
- **Lecture 6: Natural Language Processing (NLP)**
  - **Topics**: Syntax trees, Context-Free Grammar, Semantics, Recurrent networks for text, Transformers (briefly).
  - **Decision**: **Skipped** (as per your plan). *Note: You can always return to this later when using pre-built libraries like Hugging Face, where the heavy math is abstracted away.*
